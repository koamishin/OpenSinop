---
title: Record and review collections
description: Record an official receipt or collection report, then have another staff member check the amount and restrictions.
---

Use **Collections** when the institution has already received money and you need a
reviewed record of it. A collection can be an aggregate cashier report; you do not
need individual student accounts to start.

**Result:** a saved collection with a review status. No new money is received and no
bank balance changes when you record it.

## Before you start

Have an official receipt or completed report, its coverage dates, and the total received.
Know how much must be set aside, such as donor restrictions or scholarship money.
An invoice, pending transfer, or expected tuition is not a received collection.

You need a verified finance/admin account authorized to record collections.
Independent review requires a different verified admin or super admin.

## Record a collection

1. Open **Collections** and choose **Record collection**.
2. In **Receipt**, select the payment channel and copy the receipt or report number.
   Upload the source document. The screen accepts PDF, JPG, PNG, CSV, and plain text
   files, up to 10 MB, and keeps the upload private.
3. Tell the reviewer where received funds can be confirmed. Use a separate bank entry
   or register reference if the uploaded document does not establish receipt.
4. In **Amount & dates**, use the currency printed on the source. Enter amounts
   without commas. **Amount set aside** is the restricted part of the total, not an
   extra amount. Use a completed coverage period, not future dates.
5. In **Check & save**, confirm that these receipts are not already counted elsewhere,
   inspect the totals, and choose **Save for review**.

The record appears in **Collection history** as **Awaiting review**. Saved records
cannot be edited on this screen, so check the source and dates before saving.

## Review a collection

For a manual collection, a different authorized reviewer opens **Review** and compares
the displayed evidence with the original register, bank entry, or report.

Choose one decision:

| Decision | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Approve receipt record** | The received amount, coverage, and restrictions match the evidence. |
| **Needs clarification** | Something needs investigation before the record can support planning. |
| **Reject receipt record** | The record should not be used as received cash evidence. |

Write what you independently checked and why, then choose **Save review**. Review is
final on this screen; a held or rejected record cannot later be approved here. Ask an
operator to arrange a reviewed correction rather than recording the same money again.

## Example: money set aside

A cashier report includes fees and a donor contribution reserved for student aid.
Record the full received amount and include the donor contribution in **Amount set
aside**. The reviewer checks both figures. Approval does not turn the restricted part
into money available for ordinary department bills.

## Student pilot receipts are different

**Reconcile student payment** brings an eligible, already settled student tuition or
bill payment into Collections. It is not a second receipt of money. Any overpayment
remains separately identified.

:::caution[Receipt review is not spending authorization]
Only the actual independent finance reviewer may approve a receipt. The read-only
bridge cannot supply reviews, permissions, MFA or vendor transfers. A success message
for receipt reconciliation is not permission to spend. Student-controlled planning proof
integration remains incomplete; see [Current limits](../../institution/safety-readiness/#read-only-bridge-and-assistance-boundaries).
:::

## If a record is refused

- **Already recorded:** check the document, source reference, and existing history before trying again.
- **Overlapping report period:** check whether the same channel's receipts are already covered by another batch.
- **Review unavailable:** confirm you are not the person who recorded the batch and that you have review access.

Do not change the reference or channel just to bypass duplicate checks. That can count
the same receipts twice.
